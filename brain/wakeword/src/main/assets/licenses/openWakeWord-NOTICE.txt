"Hey Anu" (ADR-121) uses two models from openWakeWord v0.5.1 (https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord),
Apache License 2.0, copyright David Scripka:

- wake_embedding.tflite is openWakeWord's embedding_model.tflite, unchanged. It re-implements Google's
  speech_embedding TFHub module, which Google provides under the Apache License 2.0.
- wake_melspectrogram.tflite is openWakeWord's melspectrogram.tflite with its input fixed at [1, 1760]
  samples and its output at [1, 1, 8, 32], because LiteRT's Kotlin CompiledModel can't resize an input.
  Its weights and operations are unchanged. scripts/wake-models.py makes it and checks that its output
  matches the original's.

openWakeWord's pre-trained wake word models (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) are not used.
