## Vision

### 10x Check
The Gallery shows on-device AI one tile at a time (chat, image, audio, skills), each starting from zero. Anu's
10x is composition: one conversation that sees, hears, knows the user, acts with approval and proves what left
the phone, on the same model and phone. Parity work feeds the layer Anu already has (memory, the Tool Broker,
the Privacy Ledger) instead of sitting beside it.

### Platonic Ideal
You hold the power button over a recipe app and say "can I make this tonight?". Anu is already warm. It sees the
recipe on screen, knows you're vegetarian and that you have a 7 pm call, and answers in two seconds on the
phone: "Yes, swap the chicken for tofu; it takes 40 minutes, so start by 6:10." It offers to add tofu to your
shopping note, and asks first. Long-press the answer and it shows the model, the two facts it used, that nothing
left the phone, and how fast it was. On a hot afternoon it quietly uses the lighter model so it never stutters.

## Reviewer Concerns
Three adversarial reviews: 5/10, 5/10, 6/10 (FAIL each; the loop stops at three). Fixes from all three were applied; those from review 3 are unreviewed. Unresolved:
- **G1's governing bar (plan, G1 and GPU-1).** Pro can't beat reading, writing and loading at once on today's numbers: the GPU reads 4x faster but writes about 15% slower and reloads in 10.6 to 21.6 s against 1.6 s. The plan makes first-word time govern; GPU-1 must put the trade-off to the user.
- **Editable assistant prompt (plan, G7 and LAB-1).** D1's grid listed an editable system prompt; ADR-033 keeps the assistant's prompt stable for the cache. LAB-1 must ask.
- **Real-app bars (plan, G15 and EVAL-1).** G5's JavaScript skills, G6's Mobile Actions and timing from tapping Send need the Gallery app installed on the owner's phone; without it they stay unmeasured.
- **Media defaults (plan, MEDIA-2 and MEDIA-3).** Keeping text derived from pictures out of cloud turns, and keeping photos encrypted only while inside the conversation window, follow MEDIA-1 but weren't asked; they're pending decisions.
